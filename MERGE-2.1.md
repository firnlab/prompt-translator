## 3. FINAL merged design for the owner

### A. What PT 2.1 is
PT stays one HTML page for two logins. Its job is your verified goal: real, running digital tools of any kind, as automated as possible, you as the hands only where physics or your rules require it. 2.1 adds one thing to 2.0.0: **a loop with a record**, the same loop for a tool (in the page, per project) and for PT itself (in the repo).

**The record.** For a tool: the goal card (`p.goal20`, your Product Goal, with the level you ask for: Usable / Running incl. on-command / Measured), the facts book (`p.ledger`: your never/must rules, facts, decisions — the rules channel, in every prompt, kept short), the solution map (`p.map20`, the backlog of routes), and one new object `p.sprint21` = the sprint card and its result log (what ran, when, read-back result, units spent, retrospective line). Run details stay in the existing hands log (`p.hands20log`). The morning card is a *view* computed from these, never a store. Rule: if it isn't written there, it didn't happen; receipts prove it was written. For PT: the repo is the record — CLAUDE.md (rules), BACKLOG.md (the master list, your order; PLAN-2.1.md renamed), STATE.md generated, one decisions file, releases.json.

**The loop, one card or one click per event.** 1 *Backlog*: the map, each route a **tool card** (route + 9 lines: needs Claude to run, needs Mac on, PT can set up fully/N min/no, who uses it, duplicate-run guard, attention per week, terms incl. multiple accounts, usage per run, where data lives), lines filled by code from PT's knowledge, ordered by you, proposed by PT. Ideas from Chat, Pick, issues, alerts and Improve-PT go in with one click, never into a running sprint. Blocked routes carry the way-around search result (Adopt → same service official way → other service → other home → other shape → smallest step for your hands → not yet, watching). Terms and your rules are hard lines: allowed / grey / not allowed with the risk; you may accept grey at your own risk, written as a decision (never buyers' data or messages to third parties, no second-account advice). 2 *Planning / GO*: you pick one card; PT shows the sprint card: goal "X runs", ≤7 steps typed PT step / build job / your hands / your click, estimate to finish (units and days, each next to its last measured value), length (3 days tool, 7 days PT release). You say GO (= today's "Take this", one path, makes a restore point). 3 *Sprint*: PT runs the steps; a *your hands* step shows the "your hands" card (today's contract card); a *your click* step is today's final-button stop. Nothing automatic changes the project or the sprint; spending follows the budget gate (under budget: quick watches run once, listed with cost; over budget: every call is a button). Blocked → way-around inside the sprint or "stuck / waiting on you". 4 *Increment*: the tool at its level with a result-log entry; for PT the published version with its gate receipt. 5 *Review*: you try it (for PT: the preview) and click keep / change / drop. 6 *Retrospective*: one line. Daily = the morning card, owner-only, recomputed from the record: sprint state per project, "waiting on you" (hands steps need the desktop app), alerts, PRs, issues. Stop rule: three sprints without something running → PT stops and says so (per project; not a spending cap).

**Estimate, continue, pause.** No cap. The sprint card always shows spent so far and estimate to finish, each next to its last measured value; the first sprints *create* those measurements. Every AI button names its cost. Continue / Pause are yours; a paused sprint resumes at the same step. The 400 units/week default is recalibrated once by measuring a normal Max week.

**Definition of Done, one each, no exceptions.** Tool: the level the goal card asks for is reached once for real + a result-log entry PT wrote after a read-back shown to you. PT: full gate FULL green on the released commit (with fingerprints) + preview published + your keep click + main published + releases.json PR merged + receipt + one retrospective line.

### C. Conflicts settled (one line each)
1. Protected sprint vs automatic jobs: nothing automatic *changes* a project or sprint; *spending* follows C2 as GO'd (under budget quick watches once, listed; over budget buttons).
2. One place for state: ledger (rules/decisions) + `p.sprint21` (sprint, log) + `hands20log` (runs); `p.decisions` and `morning20` are views/caches.
3. "Developers decide how" vs your final buttons: PT decides how within the GO'd card; final buttons, keys, installs, GO, merges are typed steps; you change a running sprint only by Pause/Drop.
4. Pick questions vs planning: refinement only; planning is one card.
5. Company kit vs Scrum roles: roles belong to the tool; founder gate ≡ GO.
6. 150 items vs KISS / the 5 Nov "all in one update": the master list is PT's backlog; each PT sprint = a goal-sized slice + full gate; L8 becomes "small releases, each a sprint" (needs your fresh GO, F1).
7. DoD vs today's gate: gate made honest first (J1, J2, J7, J9); your preview try = Sprint Review (CLAUDE.md:8 change, F2).
8. Managed Agents: one deployment per tool, own budget, after K3, no-login jobs only; the one API key is shared infrastructure shown on the card.
9. Tool data in a tool's own private repo: your decision per tool; one CLAUDE.md line (F7).
10. "Full Scrum" vs levels 1–4: called "PT's Scrum, levels 1–4", deviations named in the changelog.
11. Stop rule vs no cap: stops the loop for that project and asks; not a spending cap.
12. "Running" vs on-command: on-command up to the final button counts when the goal card says so.
13. Second owner: PO of her own workspace; her Daily = the Board on open; hands/build-job steps shown as manual; G5 before she opens 2.x.
14. Result log location: `p.sprint21.log`, never the ledger.
15. Fact expiry: one rule, the ledger date (90 days; 30 for prices).
